## Brand Identity: COVENANT

**Tagline:** "Become what you were called to be"

COVENANT is a brand for men who are preparing themselves — building character, discipline, and identity. It is not a clothing brand, not a fitness brand, not a lifestyle brand. It is a reminder. Every element of this site must carry that weight.

---

## Tone & Voice

- Serious, deep, masculine. Never generic motivation.
- Every word must feel chosen with purpose — as if it costs something to say it.
- The brand speaks like a vow, not a sales pitch.
- Copy should feel like responsibility, not inspiration.

**Never write or generate copy that sounds like:**
- A gym brand ("crush your goals", "push your limits", "beast mode")
- A fashion brand (trends, aesthetics, collections)
- An Instagram motivation account ("be your best self", "level up", "you got this")
- Generic self-help ("unlock your potential", "transform your life")

**The brand speaks to men who are preparing, not men who need encouragement.**

---

## Visual Design Rules

### Colors
- Primary palette: Black, charcoal gray (`#1a1a1a`, `#2b2b2b`), off-white/broken white (`#f0ede8`)
- Accent: Deep gold (`#b8960c`, `#c9a84c`) or dark red (`#8b1a1a`, `#7a0000`) — used sparingly
- **Never use Tailwind's default blues, greens, or bright colors**
- Depth over flatness: layered backgrounds, subtle textures, dark gradients

### Typography
- **Titles:** Serif or strong display typeface (e.g., Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or a condensed serif with weight). Never thin, never casual.
- **Body:** Clean, legible sans-serif (e.g., Inter, DM Sans). Distinct from the title font.
- **Never use the same font family for both titles and body text.**
- Letter-spacing on headings: slightly wide for gravitas. Body text: normal.

### Design Philosophy
- Never generic. Every layout decision should reinforce the brand's weight.
- Prefer visual depth: layered elements, subtle grain textures, dark overlays on images.
- Whitespace is intentional, not filler.
- Imagery (if used): raw, serious, purposeful. No stock-photo energy.
